# Do not become a Betaflight configurator

The Quest-Native Flight Station may select and use already-configured drone functions such as arming and Drone Flight Mode through control channels, but it does not configure the drone itself. Betaflight configuration, firmware flashing, and bench setup remain outside the Live Pilot Station boundary. A stock MSP tunnel probe is allowed as command-ingress research only; it does not make Betaflight configuration part of the normal product workflow.
