# Make spatial control the long-term differentiator

Stick Control Scheme is the first real-flight proof and remains a useful fallback, but Natural Spatial Control is the long-term differentiator for the Quest-Native Flight Station. Spatial Control Schemes should be explored as potentially more natural, embodied, or learnable than a traditional FPV radio for short-range ExpressLRS micro FPV flights, while accepting that the right scheme will require experimentation in either simulator or real-drone Session Environments.
